## 🛒 RINGING UP A SALE (Most Common Task)

**Step-by-Step:**

1. Click **"POS"** button on main menu
2. Scan or search for each item the customer wants
3. Enter quantity they're buying
4. System shows: Item name, price, tax (if applicable), line total
5. Review total amount to charge
6. Select payment method:
   - **Cash** → Customer pays exact amount
   - **Card** → Swipe card or enter details
   - **Credit** → System remembers they owe us
   - **Mixed** → Partly cash, partly credit
7. Click **"Save Sale"**
8. Click **"Print Receipt"** (thermal or A4 invoice)
9. Hand receipt to customer
10. ✅ Done!

**Common Speeds:**

- 1-2 items: 30 seconds
- 5 items: 2-3 minutes
- 10+ items: 5 minutes

## 💳 DIFFERENT PAYMENT TYPES

### Cash

- Customer counts cash
- You receive money
- System records "Cash" payment
- Transaction complete

### Card

- Customer provides card
- You process card payment
- Insert/tap card as needed
- Keep card receipt
- System records "Card" payment

### Credit ("On Account")

- Customer can't pay now
- You select "Credit" as payment
- System adds to what they owe
- Next time they visit, remind them
- When they pay, select "Cash" and apply to their account

### Mixed (Common for Big Orders)

- Customer pays Rs. 5,000 now (cash)
- Owes Rs. 5,000 (credit)
- Select **"Mixed"** payment type
- Enter cash amount: 5,000
- Credit amount: 5,000
- System tracks both

## 🔢 TAX (VAT) EXPLANATION

**What is VAT?**

- Government tax added to some items
- Usually 15% in Sri Lanka
- Example: Item costs Rs. 100 → with VAT = Rs. 115

**How System Handles It:**

- Some items are marked "VAT Applicable"
- System automatically adds tax
- **You don't need to calculate** - system does it
- On receipt, shows: Subtotal + VAT = Total

**Which Items Have VAT?**

- Ask your manager
- Or in Inventory, look for ✓ mark next to "VAT Applicable"

## 🎯 IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

| Problem                 | Solution                                  |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Can't find a product    | Search by SKU or barcode, not just name   |
| Can't find a customer   | Try phone number instead of name          |

## ⚡ QUICK TIPS

### Faster POS

- Use barcode scanner when possible (faster than searching)
- Keep phone number for repeated customers
- Use number pad for quantities (faster than mouse)

### Preventing Mistakes

- Always read the item name before confirming
- Check quantity entered is correct
- Confirm total before accepting payment
- Don't guess at prices - search for item

### Handling Lines

- If line is long, scan items fast first, then handle payments
- Keep scanning going, process payments after
- Never delay customers by searching slowly

### End of Shift

- Hand over physical cash to manager
- Tell them about any unusual transactions
- Always process daily close properly
